## Course Topics

The course covers (in rough chronological order, Sept → Jan):

- IPv4 network design;
- IPv6 protocol and network design;
- Cellular networks;
- Ethernet local area network design;
- Principles of dynamic routing;
- Virtual Private Networks (VPN);
- MPLS protocol architecture;
- Protocols and techniques for quality of service on IP networks;
- Optical networks.

The schedule interleaves the two teachers: Casetti covers IPv4, IPv6, cellular/wireless, LANs, and routing; Baldi covers VPN and MPLS.

## Exam

> [!definition] **Exam format**
> Mandatory 1-hour written exam with **33 multiple-choice questions**. Correct answer: **+1 point**. Wrong answer: **−1/3 points**. No answer: **0 points**. The final mark is rounded to the nearest integer (25 and 1/3 → 25; 25 and 2/3 → 26). **No oral exam.**

With 33 questions worth 1 point each, the maximum raw score is 33, which maps to 30 e lode under the standard Politecnico conversion; answering randomly is penalized (expected value 0 for a 3-choice question), so skipping is better than guessing when uncertain.

## Rules of Communication

When writing to the professors, always state:

- your **name and matricola number**;
- the **title of the course** (each professor teaches multiple courses) — "I am in your Networks course" is not enough.

Other rules:

- When registering for an exam and unable to attend: **unregister or send an email**, to optimize logistics and avoid printing extra exam material.
- **The exam mark is final**: emails asking for mark redress, an oral exam, an extra exam session, or registration in a different session *will not be answered*.
